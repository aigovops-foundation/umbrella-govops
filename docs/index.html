Why Umbrella

Three things every AI governance program does badly.

Umbrella-GovOps is built around the failure modes auditors actually find.

PDF PDF theater

Policies live in slide decks.

Word docs and confluence pages do not block a merge. Umbrella encodes every obligation as a YAML control with an executable check, so non-compliance is a CI failure — not a footnote.

× Framework sprawl

NIST, EU AI Act, ISO 42001 — duplicated everywhere.

Teams maintain three parallel spreadsheets. Umbrella's Unified Control ID pivots map one control to every framework, so adding ISO 42001 is a crosswalk edit, not a re-implementation.

∅ Unverifiable evidence

"Trust us, we ran the tests."

Umbrella signs every bundle with Sigstore keyless, records it in the Rekor transparency log, and ships a one-line verification command. An auditor independently confirms every claim.

The pipeline

Six stages from YAML to a signed evidence bundle.

Every push to main runs the same DAG that produces your audit pack.

01 · Ingest
Frameworks
Load NIST AI RMF + EU AI Act Annex IV as OSCAL catalogs. Schema-validate.
02 · Classify
Risk tier
Determine each system's EU AI Act tier (high / limited / minimal / prohibited).
03 · Run
Domain checks
10 domains in parallel — pytest, Python, and OPA / Rego policies.
04 · Crosswalk
UCID pivot
Resolve every result to its NIST subcategory, EU article, ISO clause.
05 · Sign
Evidence bundle
In-toto + SLSA attestation, Sigstore keyless signature, Rekor receipt.
06 · Report
Conformity
OSCAL AR + Annex IV technical doc + cross-framework heatmap.

Governance domains

Ten domains. One YAML schema. Every framework satisfied.

Each domain owns its controls, executable checks, and evidence templates.

DG

Data Governance

Provenance, bias evaluation, PII minimization. NIST MEASURE-2.11 · EU Art. 10 · Annex IV §2(d).

ML

Model Lifecycle

Training, eval, model cards, signing via OpenSSF Model Signing. NIST MAP-4 · EU Annex IV §2(a–c).

HO

Human Oversight

Operator controls, override paths, escalation. EU Art. 14 · NIST GOVERN-3.2.

TD

Transparency & Disclosure

User notice, watermarking, deployer instructions. EU Art. 13 · NIST MEASURE-2.8.

SR

Security & Robustness

Adversarial, red-team, accuracy targets. EU Art. 15 · NIST MEASURE-2.7.

LT

Logging & Traceability

Automatic event logs, retention, supply chain. EU Art. 12 · NIST MEASURE-2.8.

RM

Risk Management

Continuous, lifecycle-wide RMS. EU Art. 9 · NIST MANAGE.

PM

Post-Market Monitoring

Telemetry sweep, drift detection. EU Art. 72.

IR

Incident Response

Serious-incident disclosure packs. EU Art. 73.
