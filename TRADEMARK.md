# Trademark Policy

**Umbrella-GovOps™**, **UCID™**, **AIGovOps Foundation™**, and **AIGovOps
Beacon™** are unregistered trademarks of the AIGovOps Foundation, a U.S.
501(c)(3) nonprofit. This policy describes how the marks may be used.

> **Status:** v0.1. A formal trademark policy will be ratified by the
> Foundation Board concurrent with USPTO filings. Until then, this v0.1
> applies and is intended to be permissive while preventing source confusion.

---

## 1. What you may do without asking

- **Refer to the project by name** in articles, talks, blog posts, books,
  academic papers, conference materials, social media, and product
  documentation — including critical, comparative, and review contexts.
- **Use the marks in factually accurate descriptions** such as
  "compliant with Umbrella-GovOps v1.0", "uses the UCID crosswalk",
  "verified with AIGovOps Beacon", or "ships an Umbrella-GovOps evidence
  bundle" — provided your claim is true and verifiable against the
  published conformance suite.
- **Distribute the unmodified source code** of Umbrella-GovOps or Beacon
  under Apache-2.0 with the marks intact.
- **Use the official medallions and word marks** in slide decks, training
  materials, and community events that promote the projects, provided
  the marks are not altered.

## 2. What requires written permission

- **Selling a commercial product, service, certification, or training
  course under a name that incorporates "Umbrella-GovOps", "UCID",
  "AIGovOps Beacon", or a confusingly similar variant.**
- **Claiming Foundation endorsement, certification, or partnership** for
  any product, service, training, or person — including
  "Umbrella-Certified", "Beacon-Certified", "AIGovOps-Approved", or
  similar.
- **Using the marks as part of a domain name, app name, package name on
  a registry (PyPI, npm, crates.io, etc.), or repository name** when the
  name suggests official affiliation. For your own work that builds on
  Umbrella, please use "for Umbrella-GovOps" or "Umbrella plugin"
  phrasing rather than incorporating the mark into the lead name.
- **Modifying the medallion, word mark, or wordmark color/typography**
  beyond reasonable scaling.

Request permission at `trademark@aigovopsfoundation.org`. The Foundation
aims to respond within 14 days.

## 3. What is never allowed

- **False certification or false conformance claims.** Stating that a
  product is "Umbrella-Certified" or "Beacon-Verified" without a
  signed certificate issued by the Foundation is prohibited.
- **Misrepresenting Foundation membership, board status, working-group
  chair status, or Designated Expert status.**
- **Using the marks to disparage or in ways likely to harm the
  Foundation's reputation or the integrity of the public AI
  governance commons.**

## 4. Fair use & nominative use

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict nominative fair use,
news reporting, scholarship, parody, or commentary protected under
applicable law.

## 5. Enforcement

The Foundation prefers correspondence to litigation. We will contact
you in writing before any enforcement action.

## 6. Amendments

This policy is amended by board ratification of a PR with
`trademark-change` label, after a 14-day public comment window.
